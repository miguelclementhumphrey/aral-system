Log in by selecting the school name, then use the School ID as the password.
Upon logging in, there are two buttons: Teachers and School Head.
Teachers cannot use their button if they are not yet enrolled by the School Head.
This means the School Head must first activate their account.
The Teachers’ button remains deactivated if no grade level has been created and no teachers have been added yet.
To activate the account, the School Head must log in through the School Head button.
After entering the button, the School Head must complete their profile setup (refer to School Head profiling).
Once the School Head profile is completed, the Create Grade Level feature will be activated.
In the Create Grade Level section (Kinder, Grades 1–12, Floating):
Example: If the School Head creates “Kinder,” they can then add teachers.
When adding teachers, only the full name is required (First Name, Middle Name, and Last Name).
Once all teachers have been added, the Teachers Button will be activated.
After activation, teachers can log in.
The grade levels created by the School Head will automatically appear.
Teachers will enter the grade level where they are assigned.
Learners cannot be added unless the teacher has completed profiling first.
Therefore, the teacher must complete profiling before adding learners. (Refer to profiling.)
When adding learners, each learner must also be profiled individually. (Refer to learners’ profiling.)
There is an ARAL LEARNER button once a child is identified as an ARAL learner.
When the teacher clicks it, it will automatically direct them to the ARAL Dashboard.
This means every grade level created will automatically have its own ARAL Dashboard.
When the ARAL Dashboard is opened:
The enrolled learners will be displayed.
Each learner will have an Update Data button.
In updating the data:
Additional profiling information will be completed.
(This refers to the violet-highlighted sections.)
There are default sections for:
Weekly Attendance
Monthly Reading Level
